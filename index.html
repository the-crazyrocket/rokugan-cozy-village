Welcome to the village

A quiet island in Rokugan that follows your real clock. Grow daikon (minutes), rice (half an hour) and tea (a few hours), fish in the river and the sea, find wild greens and help Hana with her daily requests. There is no energy bar and nothing withers — take your time.

  • Tap anywhere to walk there, or tap things to do the obvious: ripe crops get harvested, dry ones watered, soil gets seeds, water to fish, Hana to shop
  • Swipe across your field to harvest, water or plant every tile you pass over · drag elsewhere to look around
  • The seed slot picks what to plant · Dig holds the hoe, axe, shovel and earth for shaping the land · saplings go in the seed slot and grow into trees in a day
  • Grow the island: in Dig, tap the sea beside the shore with the earth basket to mark out a plot of new land · stone comes from the rock outcrop and the rocks that turn up each morning
  • The ribbon under your koku offers the next chore: tap it to harvest or water the whole field
  • Tap the clock for today's requests and what's ready
  • Pinch to zoom · two fingers to look around · hold to inspect
  • Fishing: when the ! pops up, tap anywhere (or Reel!)
  • Build: pick a decoration, tap to preview, drag it, tap again or ✓ to place · tap a placed one to move / rotate / store
  • WASD/Arrows walk · click/tap to walk or use a tool on a tile
  • 1–9 pick the seed to plant · G dig (hoe, axe, shovel, earth) · Space/E do what's in front of you
  • B build mode · R rotate · click to place / pick up
  • I bag · T today · talk to Hana at the stall to sell and buy · mouse wheel to zoom
  • Drag the mouse across your field to harvest / water / plant every tile, elsewhere to look around
  • Fishing: click the water, press Space when the ! appears
  • Left stick walk · A do what's in front of you (cast, reel in, plant, chop…) · B stop
  • LB/RB pick the seed or tool · X dig kit · Y bag · ☰ today · ⧉ build
  • LT/RT zoom · right stick look around · D-pad ▲ the ribbon's chore · ▼ camera back
  • Build: stick moves the cursor · A place · X rotate · LB/RB what to place · B done
  • In cards: D-pad to move · A press · B close · LB/RB tabs · right stick scroll

Progress is saved automatically.
