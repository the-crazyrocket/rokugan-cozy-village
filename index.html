Welcome to the village

A quiet island in Rokugan. Grow rice, daikon and tea, fish in the river and the sea, and decorate as you like. There is no energy bar — take your time.

  • WASD/Arrows walk · click/tap to walk or use a tool on a tile
  • 1–7 hand, hoe, watering can, rod, seeds · Space/E use tool
  • B build mode · R rotate · click to place / pick up
  • I bag · talk to Hana at the stall to sell and buy · mouse wheel to zoom
  • Fishing: face water with the rod, press Space when the ! appears

Progress is saved automatically in this browser.
